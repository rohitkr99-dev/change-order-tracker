The problem it solves

If you manage projects, you probably track changes in a personal Excel sheet, rebuild the status report by hand every week, and find out an approval has been stuck for a month only when someone asks. This workbook keeps the log, the aging flags and the report in one place, so the report builds itself from the log.

What you get

Change order log

One row per change: status, direct cost, markup, schedule days, dates. Total cost impact and days-open calculate for you, with dropdowns for status and type.

One-page status report

Health lights, original vs. revised contract value, pending exposure, schedule impact, the five oldest open items, and boxes for your commentary. Prints to a single page.

RAID log

Risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies with an owner, due date and a probability × impact score. Overdue and high-rated items flow into the report.

Overdue flags

Set your own limit in days. Anything pending longer is flagged, so stalled approvals show up before the meeting, not during it.

Questions

Does it work in Excel and Google Sheets?

It's built and tested in Microsoft Excel. It uses only standard functions and should open in Google Sheets, but I have not tested it there yet.

Does it replace my contract or accounting records?

No. It's a tracking aid. Always check amounts and approvals against your contract documents.

How many change orders can it hold?

200 change orders and 100 RAID items per file. You can extend it by copying the last row's formulas down.

Are the example rows included?

Yes. A few example rows show the format. Delete them or type over them.
